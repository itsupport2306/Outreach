How it works

Paste a role → rank → outreach → schedule → review. Everything stays transparent and reversible.

1
Role setup
Paste the job description, set required skills/certs, and add must-ask questions.
Your role setup becomes the source of truth for ranking and interviews — no rework.
2
Rank + outreach
Run ranking, select candidates, and send SMS/email outreach with throttles & caps.
Keep control with kill-switches and max-per-run limits — built for production.
3
Scheduling
Collect availability, schedule interviews, and keep candidates on track with reminders.
No double-booking — reschedules are handled cleanly across SMS + email.
4
Review + decide
Open dashboards, view transcripts, and make decisions faster with clear summaries.
Performance + transcript views make auditing and handoffs effortless.
